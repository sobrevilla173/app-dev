# My Favorite Movie: Ice Age

*A herd of unlikely friends, a lost baby, and one very unlucky squirrel.*

---

## Overview

**Ice Age** is a *computer-animated adventure comedy* released in **2002** by **Blue Sky Studios**. It was directed by Chris Wedge, with Carlos Saldanha as co-director. During the Ice Age, a grumpy mammoth, a clumsy sloth, and a sly saber-toothed tiger team up to return a human baby to its tribe.

> A story about how an unlikely group can become a family.

## Main Characters

1. **Manny** – a serious, solitary woolly mammoth
2. **Sid** – a talkative and clumsy ground sloth
3. **Diego** – a saber-toothed tiger with a hidden soft side
4. **Scrat** – a saber-toothed squirrel obsessed with his acorn

## Why I Like It

- Funny and heartwarming at the same time
- Memorable characters
  - Scrat's chase for the acorn is my favorite part
  - The friendship between Manny, Sid, and Diego
- Good lessons about:
  - Friendship
  - Teamwork
  - Family
- ~~Boring moments~~ (there are none!)
